I²C Textbook Protocol Principles

1. START & STOP Conditions

START: High-to-Low transition on SDA while SCL is High.
STOP: Low-to-High transition on SDA while SCL is High.

2. Data Validity Rule

SDA must remain STABLE during the high period of SCL. SDA is only permitted to change when SCL is LOW.

3. 9th Clock Pulse & ACK/NACK

Transfers are 8 bits (MSB first). The receiver pulls SDA LOW during the 9th SCL clock pulse to signal ACK. Leaving SDA HIGH indicates NACK.
